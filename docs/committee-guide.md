# Putting things on the website

A short guide for Rosebank Art Centre committee members.

You do not need to know anything about computers to use this. There are only
two things you can post, and each one is a single form.

---

## Signing in

1. Go to the website and scroll to the very bottom.
2. Click **Committee login**.
3. Type your email address and your password, then click **Sign in**.

You stay signed in for a month, so you will not have to do this every time.

If it says your email and password did not match, check that Caps Lock is off
and try again. If you are still stuck, ask another committee member — everyone
on the committee has their own login, so nobody is ever locked out.

---

## Posting an event

Use this for an exhibition or a workshop.

1. Sign in, then click **Post an event**.
2. **Name of the event** — for example, `Spring Exhibition 2026`.
3. **What kind of event is it?** — click either **Exhibition** or **Workshop**.
4. **When is it?** — click the Starts box and pick the date from the little
   calendar. If it runs for more than one day, fill in Finishes as well. If it
   is only on for one day, leave Finishes empty.
5. **Where is it?** — the centre's address is already filled in. Change it only
   if the event is somewhere else.
6. **Anything you would like to say about it** — optional. You can leave it
   empty.
7. **Photos** — click **Choose photos**. You can pick more than one at a time
   by holding Ctrl (or ⌘ on a Mac) while you click.
   - The photos appear underneath in a list. The first one is labelled
     **Main photo** — that is the one people see on the Events page.
   - Use the **↑** and **↓** buttons to change the order.
   - Use **Remove** to take one out.
   - Big photos take a few seconds each. It tells you how it is getting on.
8. If the photos are of members' artwork and you would like them in the Gallery
   too, tick **Also put these photos in the Gallery**.
9. Click **Publish**.

You will see **Done ✓** and a link to look at it on the website.

---

## Adding artwork to the Gallery

1. Sign in, then click **Add artwork**.
2. Click **Choose photos** and pick your pictures — one picture for each piece.
3. A box appears under each picture. Fill in:
   - **Name of the piece** — for example, `Autumn Grace`
   - **Artist** — for example, `Michelle Bocock`
   - **When was it made?** — this year is already filled in; change it for
     older work, and add the month and day if you know them. The Gallery lists
     the newest work first. Clear the year and the piece goes under "Earlier
     work".
   You can leave any of these empty if you are not sure.
4. Click **Save to the Gallery**.

Type each artist's name the same way every time. The Gallery groups work by
artist, and `M. Bocock` and `Michelle Bocock` would show up as two people.

---

## Changing or removing something already on the website

1. Sign in, then choose **All events** or **All artwork** near the bottom of the
   page.
2. You will see everything that has ever been posted, newest first. If there is
   a lot, use the numbered pages at the bottom.
3. Next to each one:
   - **View** opens it on the website, so you can check you have the right one.
   - **Change** opens the same form you filled in, with everything already in
     it. Correct what you need and press **Save**.
   - **Remove** takes it off the website.

**Removing is safe.** It asks you first, and it names the thing it is about to
remove so you can be sure. The item does not disappear from this list — it is
marked *Not on the website* and grows a **Put back** button. Nothing you post is
ever really deleted.

To change the photos on an event, open **Change** and scroll down to the photos.
You can remove one, add more, or use the **↑** and **↓** buttons to change the
order. Remember the first photo is the one people see on the Events page.

---

## Things worth knowing

**Photos from a phone or camera are fine.** They are made smaller automatically
before they are sent, so you do not need to resize anything first.

**Nothing is lost if you make a mistake.** You can correct or remove anything
yourself — see above — and removed things can always be put back.

**It goes live straight away.** There is no "preview" step. If you are not sure
about something, the safest thing is to ask before you click Publish.

**Take your time.** Nothing times out while you are filling in the form.

---

## Who to ask

If something looks wrong on the website, or you need a login, contact the
committee member who looks after the website.
